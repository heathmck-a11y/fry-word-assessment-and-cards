Letter Hunt

How to play

  1. Line up in front of the screen.
  2. On your turn, your teacher reads what to tap: "Tap on the snake."
  3. If you see that letter in the words, tap it. Then go to the back of the line.
  4. If it's not there, tap "Not here!"
